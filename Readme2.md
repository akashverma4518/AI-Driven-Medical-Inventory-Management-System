## 4. Unit/Module Concepts Applied

- **AVL Tree:** Balanced organization and retrieval of frequently accessed medicine records.
- **Min Heap:** Identification of minimum/low-stock inventory conditions.
- **Max Heap:** Prioritization of critical, high-demand or high-priority medicines.
- **Tree Traversal:** Systematic processing of hierarchical inventory records.
- **Graph:** Representation of relationships among medicines, suppliers, categories and inventory transactions.

---

## 5. Methodology / Approach Followed

The project follows a DSA-oriented approach:

**Data Modelling → DSA Mapping → Data Structure Selection → Search/Retrieval/Prioritization → AI-Assisted Demand & Stock Analysis**

Different data structures are selected according to the required inventory operation. Heap-based ordering is used for critical, high-demand and low-stock conditions, while the initial data flow for AI-assisted demand and stock analysis has also been prepared.

## 7. Analysis / Development / Implementation

AVL-based organization is suitable for balanced search and retrieval of frequently accessed records.

Heap-based prioritization can surface critical or high-priority medicines without repeatedly scanning every record.

The DSA layer is kept complementary to the planned AI component. DSA handles organization, search and prioritization, while AI is intended for demand and stock analysis.

## 10. Challenges & Corrective Actions

### Challenge 1: Selecting the Correct Data Structure

Each inventory operation required a suitable data structure.

**Corrective Action:** Each operation was mapped to the structure that best supports it instead of forcing every DSA concept.

### Challenge 2: Handling Low-Stock and High-Priority Conditions

Low-stock and critical medicines require different types of priority handling.

**Corrective Action:** Min Heap was used for minimum-level monitoring and Max Heap for critical-priority handling.

### Challenge 3: Keeping AI and DSA Roles Clear

The project required a clear separation between the DSA and AI components.

**Corrective Action:** DSA was kept focused on organization, search and prioritization, while AI is planned for demand and stock analysis.
